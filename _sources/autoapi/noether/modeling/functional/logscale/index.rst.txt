.. py:function:: to_logscale(x)

   Turns a tensor into log scale. Log is the natural logarithm of x + 1 .

   :param x: Tensor to be transformed

   :returns: Tensor in log scale


.. py:function:: from_logscale(x)

   Turns a tensor from log scale into orginal scale.
       x = from_logscale(to_logscale(x))

   :param x: Tensor to be de-transformed from log scale. Expected to be in natural logarithm + 1.

   :returns: Tensor in orginal scale
