   .. py:attribute:: adaLN_modulation
      :type:  torch.nn.Linear | None


   .. py:method:: forward(x, c = None)

      Apply (optionally AdaLN-modulated) norm then linear projection.

      :param x: Tokens of shape ``(B, L, hidden_size)``.
      :param c: Conditioning vector of shape ``(B, hidden_size)`` when ``use_modulation=True``;
                must be ``None`` when ``use_modulation=False``. The caller is responsible for any
                upstream activation (e.g. SiLU) — this layer applies the AdaLN linear directly.

      :returns: Tensor of shape ``(B, L, patch_size**2 * out_channels)``.
