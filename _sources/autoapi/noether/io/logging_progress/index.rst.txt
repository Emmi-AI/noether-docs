.. py:class:: LogProgress(*, label, total_bytes, mb_step = 32)

   Percent-based progress logger.
   - If total is known: log at N% steps (adaptive: 20% for small files, 10% for larger).
   - If total unknown: log every `mb_step` MiB.

   :param label: A label to use, e.g. a filename.
   :param total_bytes: [Optional] The total number of bytes to log.
   :param mb_step: [Optional] The number of MiB to log.

   .. py:attribute:: total


   .. py:attribute:: step

      :value: 20
